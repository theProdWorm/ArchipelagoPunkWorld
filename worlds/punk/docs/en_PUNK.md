# PUNK

## Where is the options page?

[Right here](../player-options). It contains every option you should need, but has not been iterated upon so inquiries and suggestions are encouraged. You can leave them on the [GitHub issues page](https://github.com/theProdWorm/PunkArchipelago/issues) with the tag "options".

## What does randomization do to this game?

PUNK is already highly randomized, being a roguelike and all that. The Archipelago mod replaces certain loot caches and boss drops among other things with location checks, instead sending items to other players within the multi-world until all checks have been completed, whereby loot starts dropping like usual again.

You are expected to become incredibly powerful at an accelerated rate.
